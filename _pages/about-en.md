<span class="anchor" id="about-me"></span>

I am Guoliang Luo (骆国樑). I received my bachelor's and master's degrees from the College of Materials Science and Engineering at Jilin University. I am currently pursuing a PhD through a joint program between the School of Electronic Science and Engineering at Nanjing University and Suzhou Laboratory.

My master's research focused on machine learning-assisted screening of bismuth- and antimony-based optoelectronic semiconductors using first-principles calculations. My current research focuses on AI-assisted synthesis and characterization of spintronic devices.

My research interests include:
- Machine learning
- First-principles calculations
- Spintronics
- Memory devices
